THEME: Create a product to help you LARP as someone you are not

# Idea: networking earpiece coach

## Vision

Most people walk into networking events underprepared. They forget names, blank on good questions, fumble when someone asks for their link, and forget to follow up a week later.

This product lets you larp as the most prepared, most charismatic person in the room. It knows you, it knows who's going to be there, it whispers in your ear while you talk, and it handles the follow-up after you leave. You show up as the version of yourself who did all the homework, without doing any of it.

The journey has three stages:

1. **Before:** it learns who you are and preps you on the event and the people.
2. **During:** it listens, whispers hints, and has your links ready the moment someone asks.
3. **After:** it remembers every conversation and tells you who to reach out to and what you promised.

## Features

### 1. Knowledge base: it knows you

You give it your resume and a few links: projects, GitHub, LinkedIn, portfolio, demo. It builds a picture of who you are, what you've built, and what you're good at. Every other feature draws on this, so hints sound like you and links are always yours. No email sync.

### 2. Event + people prep: it knows them

It finds upcoming events and their guest lists, then figures out what the event is about. It ranks the attendees against your profile and surfaces the people most worth talking to for you, along with why.

Pick a few and it goes deeper, building a dossier on each: what they work on, their interests and hobbies, and what you have in common. Each dossier ends with a few openers so you never walk up with nothing to say.

### 3. Earpiece coach: it whispers what to say

The main event. During a conversation it listens through your earpiece. When the other person asks something tricky, or the conversation stalls, it whispers a short hint: an answer, a follow-up question, or a callback to something from their dossier ("ask about their tennis app"). You stay in control, and it just makes sure you never blank.

### 4. Live link cards: it's already got it ready

Because it's listening, it knows when someone asks "do you have a demo?" or "what's your LinkedIn?" Before you reach for your phone, the right link is already on screen as a card they can scan. Instant, smooth, and very obviously "this thing has been paying attention."

### 5. Follow-up tracker: it remembers for you

After the event it turns every conversation into a list of who you met, what you talked about, and what you promised. A week later it nudges you ("you told Sam you'd send the deck") and drafts the follow-up message so all you do is hit send.
